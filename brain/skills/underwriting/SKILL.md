# Underwrite the mechanism

Use when screening suggests that detailed cash flow, debt, capital work or exit
assumptions could change the investment decision. Use only host-supported financial
functions and pinned inputs; the model chooses scenarios and interprets results,
never performs arithmetic or supplies numerical guesses.

## Procedure

- State the proposed acquisition basis and investor policy. If price, costs, hold,
  financing or return hurdles are unavailable, scope questions; do not invent them.
- Bridge reported operations into supported in-place income and recurring expenses.
  Keep cash versus accrual, lender NOI, valuation NOI and investor cash definitions
  explicit. Preserve each adjustment and its evidence, not merely the net result.
- Build historical/in-place, executable business-plan and downside cases separately.
  Rent uplift requires supported scope, costs, downtime, timing, achieved comparable
  evidence and available working capital. Asking rents alone cannot fund the plan.
- Separate repairs, recurring maintenance, replacement reserves, capital spending
  and financing reserves. Bridge deposits and releases without double counting.
- Test debt service, amortization, floating-rate protection, covenants and maturity
  repayment against supported terms. Financing approval is not an equity recommendation.
- Examine interim liquidity as well as annual ratios. A delay in stabilization,
  capex overrun or refinance shortfall can fail a solvent-looking annual model.
- Choose linked downside scenarios: income pressure, expense increases, leasing delays,
  capital overruns, rate exposure and a weaker exit/refinance. Record rationale and
  supportable bounds as assumptions; do not attach invented probabilities.
- Interpret deterministic results against explicit owner policy, evidence quality and
  fragility. Keep a pass/hold available even when the return metric looks attractive.
- If a workbook is requested, use the host template, build, recalculate and check parity.
  Every material financial quantity resolves to Fact or CalcResult and its input versions.

Ask lender/appraiser/engineer/counsel for issues outside this role's competence. Cite
OCC lending, HUD MAP and interagency workout resources through knowledge_search.
Their program conventions are not universal acquisition policy.
