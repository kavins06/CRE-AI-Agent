# Diligence by investment consequence

Use to test whether the thesis is executable, financeable and lawful, not to produce
a generic checklist or professional clearance. Group dependencies by consequence,
evidence owner, deadline/expiry, missing evidence and reconsideration trigger.

- **Operations:** leases, collections, delinquency, concessions, utility obligations,
  vendor contracts, payroll, unpaid bills, management resources and transfer disruption.
  Preserve conflicts between interested-party reports and independent confirmation.
- **Physical/capital:** condition reports, inspection coverage, accessibility, systems,
  near-term repairs, reserves, bids and business-plan construction disruption. Uninspected
  areas are unknown; condition screening is not a guarantee or certified scope.
- **Environmental/health:** past use, environmental assessment scope/age/reliance,
  moisture, lead, known notices and incomplete investigations. Missing records do not
  demonstrate absence of hazards. Qualified professionals assess and clear issues.
- **Legal/use:** ownership interest, title/survey, access, permitted use, restrictions,
  leases, affordability/subsidy obligations, pending claims and fair-housing processes.
  Counsel determines legal effect; the analyst records investment consequences.
- **Insurance/taxes:** bindable coverage, exclusions, loss history, replacement value,
  reassessment/appeal assumptions and post-transfer obligations. History is not a quote.
- **Financing/closing:** actual commitments, covenants, escrows, rate protection,
  funding conditions, maturity, guaranties and executable closing dependencies.

Escalate missing evidence when it could reverse the recommendation, prevent use,
remove funding or create a material unfunded obligation. Draft targeted questions:
what evidence, who can supply it, why it matters and what happens if unavailable.
An invented contingency budget is not resolution.

Reference EPA lead, NIOSH assessment, HUD/DOJ accommodations and appraisal guidance
through canonical knowledge_search with edition/reliance limits. Do not ingest
REFERENCE_ONLY ASTM standards or treat public guidelines as current local law.
